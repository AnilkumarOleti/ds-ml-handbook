With 28,000 users per arm, two-sided 5% and 80% power: 0.71 points of conversion, a 7.1% relative lift, comfortably below the 1 point the test was sized for. On the refund rate the smallest detectable rise is 0.40 points, 13.5% relative.

The run length that suits the primary metric leaves the guardrail nearly blind: refunds could rise by a tenth without a significant result. "No guardrail is significantly worse" is weak protection.
